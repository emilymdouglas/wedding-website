Will there be a ceremony?+

We're planning to do a small, elopement-style ceremony sometime before the day of the reception, so no formal ceremony will be held during the reception event on September 18th.

What does a dress code of "European garden party" mean?+

We're going for a festive garden party feel. Think dressy, colorful, textured, and romantic styles that fit an elevated cocktail dress code. Still unsure? Check out this link for inspiration.

Is there a registry?+

Your presence is all we ask for. However, if you'd like to contribute to a house or travel fund, you can use Venmo or PayPal. Please make sure to include your name and what you think we should use the funds for (hiking the Andes? a bottle of wine in Italy? a certain household feature you could never live without?).
